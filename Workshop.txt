description=[h2]Description[/h2]
description=[i]The world ended, sure. Civilization collapsed, absolutely. But does your safehouse have to look like a grey concrete bunker built by someone who's given up on life? Not anymore.
description=Long before Dulux and Sherwin-Williams, humans were already grinding up berries, rust and burnt wood to make their caves less depressing. If the Romans could whitewash an aqueduct, you can whitewash your barricaded gas station.[/i]
description=
description=This mod lets you produce your own paint from scratch using nothing but Quicklime, water, and whatever pigment-adjacent nonsense you can scavenge, forage or scrape off an old piece of metal. No paint store required — just proper (ish) chemistry and a bucket.
description=
description=Every color is built on the same lime-based binder used since Antiquity for limewash — the same stuff that's kept Mediterranean houses white for millennia. Every pigment recipe is based on a real historical process too: Red and Turquoise are iron oxide and verdigris, made by rusting scrap metal in vinegar. Black is charcoal, the same pigment used in cave paintings 40,000 years ago. No hand-waving "grind rock, get paint" here — if you know your chemistry, you'll recognize every recipe.
description=
description=[h2]Crafting Process[/h2]
description=
description=[b]Step 1 — Slake your Lime[/b]
description=Your binder. Vanilla [b]Misc[/b] category, prep work only. Requires: 1 Quicklime, 0.6L Water, 1 Bucket (kept).
description=[i]Quicklime does not care about your safety — see [b]Hazards[/b] below before you burn your hands off.[/i]
description=
description=[b]Step 2 — Mix your Paint[/b]
description=Mix Slaked Lime directly in an empty paint bucket. Dedicated [b]Paint[/b] crafting category, no digging through unrelated recipes.
description=[list]
description=[*] [b]White[/b] — 3 Slaked Lime + 1.5L Water
description=[*] [b]Black[/b] — 2 Slaked Lime + 1.5L Water + 1 Charcoal [i](mortar & pestle)[/i]
description=[*] [b]Yellow[/b] — 2 Slaked Lime + 1.5L Water + 4 Mustard
description=[*] [b]Blue[/b] — 2 Slaked Lime + 1.5L Water + 8 Blueberries [i](mortar & pestle)[/i]
description=[/list]
description=[b]Step 3 — Blend for Secondary Colors[/b]
description=4th grade color theory:
description=[list]
description=[*] [b]Grey[/b] — White + Black (1:1)
description=[*] [b]Orange[/b] — Red + Yellow (1:1)
description=[*] [b]Cyan[/b] — Blue + Green (1:1)
description=[*] [b]Light Brown[/b] — Brown + White (3:7)
description=[*] [b]Light Blue[/b] — Blue + White (3:7)
description=[/list]
description=Mix straight into an empty bucket, or in bulk with two full buckets — no extra empty bucket needed.
description=
description=[b]Learning the recipes:[/b] Not known by default. Watch the VHS tape below, or reach it naturally: Masonry 5 + Cooking 3 for Slaked Lime, Cooking 5 for the primary colors.
description=
description=[i]Full recipe list with exact ingredients and ratios: [url=https://github.com/MeowWoem/PZMods-CraftablePaint/blob/main/CraftablePaintEdition_Reference.md#recipes]see here[/url].[/i]
description=
description=[h2]Hazards[/h2]
description=Slaking lime without the right gear now has real, cumulative consequences:
description=[list]
description=[*] [b]No rubber gloves[/b] — burns to both hands, on top of the pain. No rubber gloves on hand? Improvised Garbage Bag Gloves work too, and they're craftable from an item you'll find in the first trash can you loot.
description=[*] [b]No safety goggles[/b] — temporary blindness that fades over a few in-game days.
description=[*] [b]No mask[/b] — lung irritation, triggering coughing fits that gradually ease off as you recover.
description=[/list]
description=Gear up properly (rubber gloves, safety goggles, a mask) and none of this happens. All three are independent — mix and match your negligence at your own risk.
description=
description=[i]Full list of recognized protective items: [url=https://github.com/MeowWoem/PZMods-CraftablePaint/blob/main/CraftablePaintEdition_Reference.md#protective-equipment]see here[/url].[/i]
description=
description=Each Sandbox setting toggles independently (on by default) with min/max duration sliders. A separate option (off by default) lets leather gloves count as hand protection too — not chemically accurate, but there if you'd rather skip rubber gloves or garbage bags. God Mode instantly cures any active blindness or lung irritation.
description=
description=[h2]New Items[/h2]
description=
description=[b]Slaked Lime[/b]
description=[i]"Well, you certainly can't paint with rocks. But you can with THIS rock, technically."[/i]
description=
description=The result of dumping water on Quicklime — which, chemically, is exactly as violent as it sounds. Slaked Lime is your binder for every single paint recipe in this mod, standing in for modern chemical binders the same way it's stood in for them for the last few thousand years.
description=
description=[b]Improvised Garbage Bag Gloves[/b]
description=[i]"Not OSHA-approved. Not remotely. But your hands will thank you."[/i]
description=
description=Craft these from a garbage bag when you don't have real rubber gloves handy. Not as durable or convincing as the real thing, but plastic doesn't soak up caustic lime solution the way leather or fabric does — good enough to keep your hands intact.
description=
description=[b]VHS: Self-sufficiency Module 8[/b]
description=[i]"Well uh, gray.... Hm yes, gray."[/i]
description=
description=Find this tape in the Retail-VHS loot pool and watch it through to learn every recipe above on the spot. Same script as the presentation video linked at the top of this page.
description=
description=[h2]Mod Support[/h2]
description=[list]
description=[*] [b]Paint coverage mods[/b] — every secondary color mixing ratio is recalculated dynamically from the actual UseDelta of paint buckets, so mods that change how much surface a bucket covers won't throw off your mix proportions.
description=[*] [b][url=https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=2999595757]Wallpapers and More Paint Options[/url][/b] — if that mod is active, this one also unlocks realistic recipes for its extra shades (Magenta, Dust Blue, Camo Green, Wine, Ivory, and the rest of that mod's paint lineup), using the exact same lime-and-pigment logic as the base 15 colors. No extra pigment items to hunt down, just more colors to mix. If you don't have that mod installed, these recipes stay hidden — no clutter in your crafting menu.
description=[/list]
