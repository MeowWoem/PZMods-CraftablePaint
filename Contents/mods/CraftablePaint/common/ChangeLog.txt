[ v1.2.0 - 08.17.2026 ]
Added:
- Crafting Slaked Lime without proper protection can now burn your hands, cause temporary blindness, or irritate your lungs
- Hand burns from unprotected crafting (requires rubber gloves)
- Temporary blindness from unprotected crafting (requires safety goggles)
- Lung irritation with coughing fits from unprotected crafting (requires a mask)
- Sandbox options to enable/disable each hazard individually
- Sandbox options for min/max duration of temporary blindness and lung irritation
- God Mode now instantly cures active blindness and lung irritation, in addition to vanilla injury immunity
- In-game warning added to the Slake Lime recipe tooltip
[ ------ ]

[ v1.2.2 - 08.18.2026 ]

- New Sandbox option to allow Leather Gloves (ice hockey gloves, plain/brown/black leather gloves) as hand protection — off by default, not chemically realistic, offered purely for convenience
- Temporary blindness and lung irritation now also apply additional pain to the affected body part, on top of the existing effects
